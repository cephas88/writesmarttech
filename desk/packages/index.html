Fees

From USD 10. The number comes after the brief.

The Desk is priced to stay reachable — a short post can start from as low as ten US dollars. A dissertation chapter will not. That is why there is no cart on this website. You send the task. I read the pages, the difficulty, the sources, and the due time. Then we agree a quote.

What I look at

Word count or slide count, academic level, how dense the brief is, whether sources must be found, citation style, and whether you already have a draft for me to edit.

What “urgent” means

If the work must be back in a few hours, write the exact due time in the brief. Same-day and same-evening jobs are taken when the clock still allows a careful delivery. The quote reflects that speed.

What is always included

The written quote, work by the same specialist, a revision pass in the agreed window, a free AI report, and a free plagiarism report. No extra fee for either report.

How money moves

  1. 01

    Brief

    Send the task, rubric, and deadline. Mention urgent if it is due today.

  2. 02

    Quote

    A figure in USD, AUD, GBP, or the channel you prefer. We discuss it in the same thread.

  3. 03

    Start

    Once the agreed amount is sent, writing begins. Urgent jobs begin as soon as that lands.
