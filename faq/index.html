FAQ

Straight answers, including the ones that lose some clients.

What is WriteSmart Technologies?+

One house, two rooms. The Bots are Chrome extensions for freelance writers on Writedom and WritersHub. The Desk is direct academic tutoring — you email Cephas, not a marketplace.

Is The Desk an essay mill?+

No. I tutor, edit, review structure, and coach research. I do not write assignments for you to submit as your own, sit exams, or impersonate students.

Why email instead of ordering on the site?+

So I can read the brief as a person, you can attach files in your own mail app, and there is no checkout pretending to be a relationship. The form only prepares the email.

How do I send a rubric or draft?+

Use Open email to attach files on the brief form. Your mail app opens with the brief already written — attach there. This website cannot hold files.

How fast do you reply?+

Within 30 minutes or less.

Which countries do you work with?+

The United States, United Kingdom, and Australia are the main systems. Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, and others are welcome — pick your country on the brief, or type it.

Which subjects?+

All fields. If your module is odd, type it.

Which citation styles?+

All of them in regular use: APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, OSCOLA, AGLC, IEEE, Vancouver, AMA, and a school’s own guide.

How does payment work?+

Nothing is charged on this website. After I read your brief I send a quote and payment details by email.
