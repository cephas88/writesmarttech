Academic integrity

You stay the student. I stay the specialist.

Universities in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, and elsewhere treat contract cheating as a serious offence. The Desk is built so that does not happen here.

I provide editing, proofreading, writing tutoring, structure review, citation help, and research coaching. The aim is that you understand the work and can stand behind it.

I will not:

  • Write an assignment for you to submit as your own
  • Sit an exam, quiz, or invigilated assessment
  • Impersonate you on a portal, email, or video call
  • Guarantee a grade
  • Help conceal another person's authorship

If a brief asks for those things, I decline. You are responsible for following your institution's academic honesty policy.
