The Desk

Work I take on — and the line I will not cross.

Tutoring, editing, and coaching for academic writing. You remain the author of anything you submit. If you need a ghostwriter, this is the wrong door.

Writing tutoring

Learn the move, not just the mark-up.

One-to-one coaching over email: thesis, counter-argument, sources, and the register your marker expects.

Editing & proofreading

Your draft, made clearer — still yours.

Line and copy editing for any academic genre. Grammar, flow, claims that do not land, and a voice that still sounds like you.

Structure & argument review

The outline before the panic.

A written review of how the piece hangs together: claim, evidence, section order, what is missing.

Research coaching

Find the right sources, then use them.

Question scope, databases, reading strategy, and turning notes into a literature map.

Citation & referencing

Every major style — and your school’s own guide.

APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, OSCOLA, AGLC, IEEE, Vancouver, AMA, and the rest.

Draft feedback

A reader who will actually say what is weak.

A letter on what is working, what is padded, and what a marker will stop at. Then you revise.
