Getting Started

Installation Guide

Get up and running in just a few minutes — no technical skills required

01

Add Extension to Chrome

Click the "Get the Bot" or "Install the Bot" button for your desired product above. This will direct you to the Chrome Web Store listing.

Click "Add to Chrome" and confirm the installation when prompted by your browser.

Ensure you are using the latest version of Google Chrome for optimal performance.
02

Pin the Extension

After installation, pin the extension for easy one-click access:

  1. Click the puzzle piece icon in the top-right corner of Chrome
  2. Find the bot extension in the dropdown list
  3. Click the pin icon next to it to keep it permanently visible in your toolbar
03

Configure Your Bot

Click on the bot icon in your browser toolbar to open the settings panel:

  1. Click "Start Free Trial" or activate the bot with your licence key
  2. Configure your bidding preferences and budget thresholds
  3. Set up your filters, order types, and notification settings
04

Start Bidding!

Make sure to save your settings before clicking the Start Bidding button.

Once activated, the bot will begin monitoring and automatically placing bids on available orders based on your configured settings — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

That's it! Sit back and let the bot work while you focus on delivering great writing.
