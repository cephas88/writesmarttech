Getting Started

Installation Guide

Get up and running in just a few minutes

01

Add Extension to Chrome

Click the "Get the Bot" or "Install the Bot" button for your desired bot above. This will take you to the Chrome Web Store.

Click the "Add to Chrome" button on the Chrome Web Store page and confirm the installation when prompted.

Ensure you are using the latest version of Chrome for optimal performance.
02

Pin the Extension

After installation, pin the extension for easy access:

  1. Click the puzzle piece icon in the top-right corner of Chrome
  2. Find the bot extension in the dropdown list
  3. Click the pin icon next to it to keep it visible in your toolbar
03

Configure Your Bot

Click on the bot icon in your browser toolbar to access settings:

  1. Click start one time FREE TRIAL or activate the bot
  2. Configure your bidding preferences
  3. Set up your filters and notification settings
04

Start Bidding!

Make sure to save settings before clicking the Start Bidding button.

Once you click the Start Bidding button, the bot will begin monitoring and bidding on available orders based on your settings.

That's it! Sit back and let the bot do the work while you focus on writing.
