Getting Started

Installation Guide

Get up and running in just a few minutes — no technical skills required

01

Add Extension to Chrome

Click the "Get the Bot" or "Install the Bot" button for your desired product above. This will direct you to the Chrome Web Store listing.

Click "Add to Chrome" and confirm the installation when prompted by your browser.

Ensure you are using the latest version of Google Chrome for optimal performance.
02

Pin the Extension

After installation, pin the extension for easy one-click access:

  1. Click the puzzle piece icon in the top-right corner of Chrome
  2. Find the bot extension in the dropdown list
  3. Click the pin icon next to it to keep it permanently visible in your toolbar
03

Configure Your Bot

Click on the bot icon in your browser toolbar to open the settings panel:

  1. Activate the bot with your licence key
  2. Configure your bidding preferences and budget thresholds
  3. Set up your filters, order types, and notification settings
04

Start Bidding!

Make sure to save your settings before clicking the Start Bidding button.

Once activated, the bot will begin monitoring and automatically placing bids on available orders based on your configured settings — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

That's it! Sit back and let the bot work while you focus on delivering great writing.
Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Writedom and WritersHub bidding bots

What is the Writedom Bidding Bot?

The Writedom Bidding Bot is a Chrome extension by WriteSmartTechnologies that automatically places bids on writing orders on the Writedom platform. It monitors available orders 24/7, applies your customised filters, and bids instantly when a matching order appears — without you lifting a finger.

What is the WritersHub Bidding Bot?

The WritersHub Bidding Bot is a Chrome extension that automates bid placement on the WritersHub freelance writing platform. It monitors live orders and places bids automatically on your behalf so you stay ahead of the competition every single day.

How does the bidding bot work?

Once installed and activated with your licence key, the bot runs silently in the background of Chrome. It continuously monitors Writedom or WritersHub for new orders matching your configured preferences — budget, order type, and topic — and places a bid instantly on your behalf.

Is the bidding bot safe to use?

Yes. Your credentials stay on your device only and are never transmitted to any external server. The bot operates entirely within your browser using Chrome's secure extension framework — your account and data remain completely private.

How do I activate the bot after installing it?

After adding the extension to Chrome and pinning it to your toolbar, click the bot icon to open the settings panel. Enter your licence key, configure your bidding preferences (budget, order types, filters), save your settings, and click Start Bidding to activate.

Does the bot run 24/7?

The bot monitors and places bids continuously as long as Google Chrome is open and running on your computer. For best results, keep Chrome running in the background — the bot will work around the clock without any input from you.
